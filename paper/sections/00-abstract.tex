\begin{abstract}
We develop a non-commutative quantum probability framework for option pricing
when hedge trades move prices.
Replacing the classical commutativity assumption with a canonical commutation
relation---identified from the market's price-impact slope---yields a
closed-form pricing formula, resolves the short-maturity implied-volatility
skew divergence, and supports a convex quantum state tomography procedure.
Applied to regional bank equity options during a US banking crisis,
the framework detects systemic contagion weeks before price-based indicators
for gradually developing failures, and contemporaneously for sudden ones.
A quantum entropy index Granger-causes implied volatility across multiple
crisis cycles, complementing existing systemic risk measures.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Quantum probability; option pricing; market microstructure; implied volatility skew; systemic risk.
